## Functional issue — fix

**D5/D6 cannot be driven.** Their anodes are on `/PC3` and `/PC4`, but those nets contain only the LEDs. U2 pin 13 (PC3) and pin 14 (PC4) are explicitly no-connect. Remove those two no-connect markers and connect matching labels at the MCU. This explains the two isolated-label ERC warnings. Preserve the series resistors R16/R17.

## Power design risks — resolve before committing the layout

**PD current budget and fallback.** Actual wiring is R18 = 10 kΩ on VSET and R15 = 4.7 kΩ on ISET. Hynetek specifies 10 kΩ for 12 V and 4.53 kΩ for 1.5 A; 4.7 kΩ is not its listed nominal tap. Using the documented 15.4 W LED load, converter input alone is approximately 17.1–18.1 W at an assumed 90–85% efficiency, before controller overhead. A 12 V/1.5 A contract therefore has little or no margin; 9 V/1.5 A cannot support full output. Select a documented ISET value and sufficient contract current, then enforce a brightness limit or disable the drivers when the source cannot supply the required power. Firmware should read the negotiated contract before enabling PWM. R14 already provides a 10 kΩ CTRL pulldown.

The 12 V strap is sensible. Do not request 20 V through I2C: it overrides the resistor settings, whereas TPS61165 has an 18 V recommended input ceiling and 20 V absolute maximum. Also ensure the maximum chosen supply remains below the minimum LED-string forward voltage: the boost circuit has a direct input-to-output path even when disabled. U1's exact `HUSB238_xxxDD` suffix remains unspecified; variants differ in fallback behavior. Source: [Hynetek datasheet, tables 5/6 and I2C operation](https://www.hynetek.com/uploadfiles/site/219/news/aabbbbdb-48c9-4a44-a6dc-2c15f53282e6.pdf).

**D7/F1 protection coordination.** D7 SMAJ24A is on USB_VBUS, before F1. F1 therefore cannot interrupt current into a failed-short D7. Decide whether the intended protection is upstream current limiting or a fuse ahead of the TVS branch. A 24 V TVS does not clamp at 24 V; representative SMAJ24A specifications give 26.7–29.5 V breakdown and 38.9 V rated clamping. It does not establish a safe transient ceiling for the downstream circuitry. Select protection by actual clamping voltage/current and component absolute maxima; a lower nominal TVS alone is not proof of adequate protection. Verify the exact fitted manufacturer's data. Reference: [Littelfuse SMAJ series](https://www.littelfuse.com/~/media/electronics/datasheets/tvs_diodes/littelfuse_tvs_diode_smaj_datasheet.pdf.pdf).

**Open LED / unplugged harness conditions.** TPS61165's SW overvoltage threshold is 37–39 V. Check D1–D4's 40 V Schottky margin including ringing and hot operation; consider a higher-voltage part if losses are acceptable. C8–C11 are 50 V parts, but effective capacitance at elevated DC bias also matters. Treat powered harness reconnection as a validation case, including current transients and FB stress; prefer connecting the panel while unpowered. Source: [TI TPS61165 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps61165.pdf).

**U7 thermal budget.** It is connected to pre-switch VBUS, which allows MCU startup before the LED load is enabled. At 12 V, a 20 mA 3.3 V load dissipates approximately 174 mW in the LDO; 40 mA dissipates 348 mW. Check actual MCU/peripheral consumption and the exact HT7533 thermal limits with the available copper. Label J3/J4 clearly: their power pins connect directly to the board's 3.3 V rail, so externally supplied programmer/UART power needs an intentional connection policy.

## Controller layout

- J2 has two internal footprint hole-clearance violations: 0.2036 mm actual versus the configured 0.25 mm requirement. Check the connector drawing and manufacturer capability before modifying the footprint or applying a narrow exception. Do not relax all clearances to hide it.
- D5/D6, R9–R14, and R16/R17 remain outside the 55 × 50 mm outline. These are placement backlog. The additional J2 courtyard overhang is only 0.25 mm at the connector mouth and may be intentional.
- The four repeated boost clusters are compact, and the separation from the MCU is useful. Finish their high-current ground paths and exposed-pad connections before signal routing. DRC currently flags ground disconnections including driver exposed pads. Keep the SW–diode–output-capacitor loop short, connect input bypass returns locally, and keep FB/COMP away from SW copper. Provide a ground plane and thermal connections consistent with TI's layout guidance.
- Only Default netclass exists: 0.2 mm routing width and 0.2 mm clearance. Establish power routing sizes for the common input and ground paths based on aggregate current, copper thickness, and temperature rise. The minimum-width rule is not a current-rating check.
- Clean up six silkscreen warnings around D4, F1/D7, C10, C11, and U2/C18 after placement settles. Add useful connector pin labels and accessible GND, 3.3 V, switched VIN, PWM, and sense-node test access.
- Decide enclosure retention and connector insertion support before completing routing; neither board render shows mounting holes.

## LED panel

The netlist contains four independent five-LED series strings, with consistent electrical polarity. J1 assignments match the controller exactly: pins 1–8 = 2+, 1+, 2−, 1−, 3−, 4−, 3+, 4+. The panel has 44 routed segments, all F.Cu and 1 mm wide.

**Thermal design remains to be established.** The panel's 1 mm interconnects are not a heatsink specification. Define the aluminum-core dielectric and thermal conductivity, LED thermal land requirements, interface material, mounting pressure, and heatsink. Provide temperature measurement or thermal cutoff/derating if unattended operation warrants it; controller-IC thermal shutdown cannot measure panel temperature.

D5's third footprint pad has no electrical net, and the red LEDs use a generic two-pin symbol with a three-pad footprint. That can be correct for an isolated thermal pad, but verify the exact red LED drawing, pad polarity and center-pad connection requirements. The LED schematic does not identify exact MPNs or datasheets, so footprint-to-part compatibility and junction-temperature limits remain unverified. Do not blindly ground that center pad.

The project metadata still declares two copper layers despite the documented single-layer aluminum intent. All queried tracks are on top, so this is not evidence of unwanted bottom routing. Make the fabrication stackup and outputs explicit. Retain J1's position if it suits cable access; the placement tool's arbitrary edge-distance deduction is not a defect for this vertical connector. Add cable strain relief and panel retention.

## ERC and documentation cleanup

The five ERC errors concern undriven power pins. Correct U7's unspecified electrical pin types and add justified power flags at externally/passively fed supplies. The switch pin-type warnings similarly reflect library metadata. Do not blanket-disable ERC.

Automated missing-capacitor reports for U2 VCAP and U1 VIN are false positives: C16 = 1 µF and C1 = 1 µF are connected. Do not replace C16 with the audit's generic 100 nF suggestion: ST specifies 470–3300 nF for VCAP. [STM8S003 datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/pio/datasheet/stm8s003f3.pdf).

U3 pin 5 is deliberately unconnected; that is not a missing supply bypass to fix automatically. In particular, do not tie USBLC6-2's VBUS pin to the 12 V PD rail; its internal protection is intended for a low-voltage bus. Validate the chosen CC protection arrangement for the desired ESD and short-to-VBUS behavior. [ST USBLC6-2 datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/usblc6-2.pdf).

The existing BOM/block diagram need reconciliation: R1 is the VIN filter resistor, R2/R3 are the gate network, R18/R15 set voltage/current, U7 is upstream of Q1, and D7 is upstream of F1. The BOM still lists absent R8 and omits present R18. The current 0.82 Ω sense resistors give about 244 mA; the documented nominal LED load is 15.4 W rather than 20 W of LED output.

Suggested order: restore status-LED connections; settle contract current, protection and exact parts; settle heatsink/mechanics; complete power/ground routing and remaining placement; then rerun ERC/DRC and validate startup, dimming, fallback and temperatures on hardware.
